= UC-06: Create and Resolve Hold

== Primary Actor

* Sabine, Member

== Trigger

* A member requests a hold for a category so they may borrow an item once one becomes available.

== Main Success Scenario

. The member selects a category and requests a hold.
. The system checks whether the member is suspended.
. The system creates a queue entry for the category ordered by receipt time.
. The system returns the member’s queue position.
. When an item becomes available after inspection, the system checks the first member in the queue.
. If the next member is eligible, the system creates a reservation for that member.
. The reservation expires after three calendar days.
. If the reservation expires, the system advances the queue or leaves the item available.
. The system confirms the hold or reservation outcome to the member.

== Extensions

* 2a. The member is suspended.
  The hold is accepted but the member cannot collect the item. Their queue position is retained.
* 6a. The first member in queue is suspended.
  The reservation is skipped and the queue remains unchanged.
* 7a. The reservation expires.
  The system moves to the next queue member or marks the item available.
* 5a. The item becomes due for maintenance after inspection.
  No reservation is created and the queue remains unchanged.

== Postconditions

* The category queue is preserved in order.
* A reservation is valid for up to three calendar days.
* A suspended member keeps queue position but cannot collect.

== Business Rules

* BR-01: A hold applies to a category, not an individual item.
* BR-02: Holds form a queue ordered by time of receipt.
* BR-03: If an item becomes available after inspection and a hold exists, the first queue member receives a reservation.
* BR-04: A reservation expires after three calendar days.
* BR-05: A suspended member may place a hold but may not collect the item.
* BR-06: A suspended member’s reservation is skipped without removing their queue place.

== Acceptance Criteria

[source,gherkin]
----
Scenario: valid hold is created
  Given a member is eligible to place a hold
  When the member requests a hold for a category
  Then the system records the hold in queue order
  And the system returns the member’s queue position

Scenario: suspended member retains queue position
  Given a member is suspended
  When the member places a hold
  Then the hold is accepted
  And the member cannot collect the item
  And the member keeps their queue position

Scenario: expired reservation advances queue
  Given a reservation for a member has existed for three calendar days
  When the expiration threshold is reached
  Then the system advances to the next queue member or marks the item available
----
