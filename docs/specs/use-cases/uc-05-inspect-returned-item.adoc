= UC-05: Inspect Returned Item

== Primary Actor

* Rolf, Workshop Technician

== Trigger

* A returned item is available for inspection after it has been received at the counter.

== Main Success Scenario

. The technician selects the next returned item.
. The system displays the item, the active loan, the return irregularities, and previous inspection history.
. The technician records the inspection result:
   - available
   - due for maintenance
   - retired
. The system creates an inspection report and closes the loan.
. The system determines whether the item should be released, deducted, or retained according to deposit rules.
. The system writes a deposit transaction for deduction, release, or retention.
. The system updates the item state and the item’s usage counter when the outcome requires it.
. The system confirms completion to the technician.

== Extensions

* 3a. The item has no damage.
  The item becomes available after inspection.
* 3b. The item has reached the maintenance threshold.
  The item is marked due for maintenance and not available for issue.
* 3c. The item is lost or beyond repair.
  The item is retired and the deposit is retained in full.
* 4a. Damage is recorded from a previous loan.
  The system attributes the damage to the earlier loan and does not charge the current loan.
* 5a. The deduction would exceed the collected deposit.
  The system caps the deduction at the collected deposit.

== Postconditions

* The loan is closed by the inspection report.
* The item enters one of the valid states: available, due for maintenance, or retired.
* The deposit ledger records the result.
* Any overdue suspension may end once all overdue loans for the member have been inspected.

== Business Rules

* BR-01: The inspection report closes the loan and determines any deposit deduction.
* BR-02: Damage is attributed to the current loan unless it was already recorded in an earlier inspection report.
* BR-03: An item becomes due for maintenance after inspection if the usage threshold has been reached.
* BR-04: A deduction may not exceed the collected deposit.
* BR-05: A lost item retains the full deposit and is retired.
* BR-06: Every deposit transaction is written with timestamp, amount, and trigger.

== Acceptance Criteria

[source,gherkin]
----
Scenario: item inspected without damage becomes available
  Given a returned item has no damage
  And the item has not reached its maintenance threshold
  When the workshop technician completes inspection
  Then the loan closes
  And the item is marked available
  And the deposit is released if required

Scenario: item reaching maintenance threshold becomes due for maintenance
  Given the item has reached the category maintenance threshold
  When the workshop technician completes inspection
  Then the loan closes
  And the item is marked due_for_maintenance
  And the item is not available for issue

Scenario: exceeding deposit deduction is capped
  Given a collected deposit of 50 euros
  And the inspection report decides on a deduction of 70 euros
  When the deduction is applied
  Then the deduction is capped at 50 euros
  And the loan is closed
----
