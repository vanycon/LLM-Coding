== Problem

The "Library of Things" association lends tools and equipment to its members.
Rotary hammer, projector, cargo bike, chainsaw, sewing machine.
Around four hundred items, eight hundred members, open on Tuesdays and Saturdays.

Today, this is managed with a binder and a cash deposit.
The counter is staffed by volunteers, with different people each time.
On Saturday mornings, twenty people queue while someone leafs through the binder.

Three things regularly go wrong.
Deposits are paid back incorrectly because nobody knows what was deposited.
Equipment breaks because nobody noticed that maintenance was due.
And someone who reserved a device finds the doors locked because somebody else extended their loan.

== Goals

. The counter staff can tell at any time whether an item may be issued.
. Every deposit transaction is traceable.
. Equipment due for maintenance is not issued.
. A hold is honored.

=== Non-Goals

* No cash management.
  How the cash gets into the drawer is up to the association.
* No online shop.
  Issuance takes place at the counter, not by shipping.
* No member management.
  Membership fees, joining, and leaving remain outside the system.

== The Domain

A library of things works like a library — up to the point where it does not.

*Items wear out.*
A book survives a hundred loans; a rotary hammer does not.
After a certain number of loans, a device must be serviced.
Until then, it may not be issued, even if it is undamaged on the shelf.

*Return is not completion.*
Someone returning a chainsaw hands it in for inspection.
Only once someone has inspected it is the process complete — and only then is it determined whether the deposit is fully refunded.

*The deposit is not a price.*
It is based on the replacement value and is deposited when the item is issued.
It is fully refunded when the item is returned in good condition.
The association earns nothing from it.

*Some devices are not for everyone.*
Anyone wanting to borrow a device requiring instruction needs documented instruction.
That instruction belongs to the person, not the item.

== What This System Is Not

This boundary is binding.
It rules out assumptions that are natural for a lending application but incorrect here.

No library system::
A returned item does not become available immediately.
It goes into inspection.
The state "available" is not the opposite of "on loan".

No sale and no fee::
The deposit is not a price, charge, or revenue.
It is a safeguarded amount that must be returned.
Loans are free of charge.

Not merely an item property::
Whether an item may be issued also depends on the member: suspension, instruction, and number of active loans.
Checking the item alone is incomplete.

Not a calendar interval::
Maintenance due status arises from use, not from the passage of time.
A device that has stood on the shelf for a year is not due for maintenance.

Not an appointment calendar::
A hold does not reserve a period or a date.
It is a place in a queue.

== Personas

=== Karim, Counter Clerk

A volunteer, on duty for three hours every other Saturday.
He wants to know within thirty seconds whether he may issue a device and not have to calculate the deposit in his head.

=== Sabine, Member

Renovating her apartment, she borrows regularly.
She wants to see in advance whether a device is free and be able to place a hold on it.

=== Rolf, Workshop Technician

Inspects, repairs, and retires items.
He decides on deposit deductions.
He does not see the devices until days after they are returned.

== Business Rules

The rules are formulated in domain terms.
Assigning BR IDs, breaking them down into atomic rules, and mapping them to use cases belong in the specification.

=== Catalog

* Every item has a unique inventory number and belongs to exactly one category.
* The category determines the loan duration in days, the maintenance interval in loans, and whether instruction is required.
* Every item has a replacement value in euros.
* The deposit is 20 percent of the replacement value, rounded up to the next whole euro, with a minimum of 5 and a maximum of 100 euros.
* The system owns member state: suspension, active-loan count, instruction status, and hold eligibility are determined by the system, not by external caller input.

=== Loan

* An item is issued only if it is available or reserved for this member.
* A member may have at most three simultaneous loans.
  Holds do not count toward this limit.
* A suspended member may not borrow.
* An item requiring instruction may only be issued to a member with documented instruction for this category.
* When the item is issued, the deposit is collected and the return deadline is set: issue date plus the category's loan duration.
* An extension by the full loan duration is possible once per loan.
* An extension is excluded if an open hold exists for the category or the loan is already overdue.
+
Reason: Otherwise, the first borrower can keep the item indefinitely, making the hold worthless.

=== Return and Inspection

* Returning an item at the counter moves it into inspection.
  It does not end the loan.
* At return, the counter staff record irregularities but do not decide on the deposit.
* The workshop technician carries out the inspection and creates an inspection report.
+
Reason: Equal treatment.
The person facing the member should not decide about their money.
* The inspection report closes the loan and determines the deposit deduction.
* Damage is attributed to the current loan unless it was already recorded in the inspection report of an earlier loan.
+
Reason: The inspection report is the only reliable source.
Anything else would be one person's word against another's.
* After inspection, the item enters exactly one of these states: available, due for maintenance, or retired.

=== Deposit

* The deposit is collected when the item is issued and is fully or partially released with the inspection report.
* A deduction may not exceed the collected deposit.
* If an item is considered lost, the deposit is retained in full and the item is retired.
  Any claim beyond this arises outside the system.
* Every deposit transaction — collection, deduction, or release — creates an entry with a timestamp, amount, and trigger.

=== Maintenance

* Every completed loan increases the item's usage counter by one.
* When the counter reaches the category's maintenance interval, the item becomes due for maintenance after inspection instead of available.
+
Reason: Use can be counted; nobody at the counter can measure operating hours.
* After maintenance has been completed, the workshop technician sets an item due for maintenance to available and resets its usage counter to zero.
* An item due for maintenance is neither issued nor reserved.

=== Hold

* A hold applies to a category, not to an individual item.
* Holds for a category form a queue ordered by time of receipt.
* If an item becomes available after inspection and a hold exists, it is reserved for the first member in the queue.
* A reservation expires three calendar days after it is created.
  The item then goes to the next member in the queue or becomes available.
* If an item becomes due for maintenance after inspection, the queue remains unchanged.
  No reservation is created.
* A suspended member may place a hold but may not collect the item.
  A reservation created for a suspended member is skipped; the member retains their place in the queue.
+
Reason: The suspension is intended to encourage return, not to cost the member their place in the queue.

=== Suspension

* A member is suspended as soon as at least one of their loans is overdue.
* Overdue means: the return deadline is before today's date and the item has not been returned.
* The suspension ends when inspection has been completed for all of the member's overdue loans.

The implemented rules for release 1 are:

* A member is suspended immediately when a loan becomes overdue and not returned.
* Returned items move to inspection and become unavailable until inspection completes.
* A hold is category-based and queue-ordered by receipt time.
* Deposit collection and release are recorded as immutable, timestamped transactions.
* A deposit is rounded up to the next whole euro and never exceeds the collected deposit.

== Quality Goals

[cols="1,3,2"]
|===
|Goal |Scenario |Response Measure

|Security
|A member without instruction attempts to borrow a device requiring instruction through every available interface.
|No invocation results in an item being issued. Demonstrated by one automated test per interface.

|Deposit correctness
|For a member with 50 completed loans, the deposit balance is recalculated from the transactions.
|Deviation: 0 cents.

|Traceability
|The workshop technician completes an inspection with a deduction.
|The operation creates an audit entry with timestamp, amount, trigger, and a reference to the inspection report.

|Response time
|The counter checks whether an item can be issued with 400 items and 800 members.
|Response in under 300 ms, measured at the 95th percentile over 100 calls.
|===

Availability is not a quality goal.
The association opens twice a week; an outage between opening days has no consequences.

== Constraints

Interfaces::
A REST API for all use cases.
Additionally, a CLI for the workshop technician's tasks.
No web interface.

Reason::
Without a UI, the compiler and test suite provide full error correction.
Visual consistency could not be checked automatically.

Technology::
Language, framework, and persistence are freely selectable.

Operation::
A single process, startable locally, without external services.
No email delivery, payment integration, or cloud.

Authentication::
Roles are supplied via a header or CLI flag and are not checked.
Actual login is explicitly not part of this system.

Time::
The system reads the current date through a replaceable source so that deadlines can be tested.

Language::
Technical terms in the code follow the glossary in this document.

== Glossary

These terms apply unchanged in the specification, architecture, and code.

[horizontal]
Item:: A single physical specimen with its own inventory number.
Category:: A group of similar items. Determines loan duration, maintenance interval, and whether instruction is required.
Member:: A person who is allowed to borrow.
Counter Clerk:: A role that issues and accepts returned items.
Workshop Technician:: A role that inspects, services, retires items, and decides on deposit deductions.
Loan:: A process from issuance until completion of inspection.
Return:: The handover of an item to the counter. Does not end the loan.
Inspection:: Examination of an item after its return.
Inspection Report:: The result of an inspection. Closes the loan.
Deposit:: An amount collected when an item is issued. Not a fee.
Replacement Value:: The price of an equivalent replacement device.
Usage Counter:: The number of completed loans since the last maintenance.
Due for Maintenance:: A state in which an item is not issued or reserved.
Retired:: The final state of an item. Lost or beyond repair.
Hold:: A place in a category's queue.
Reservation:: An item assigned to a member for three calendar days.
Instruction:: Documented training of a person for a category.
Suspension:: A member's state when they have an overdue loan.
