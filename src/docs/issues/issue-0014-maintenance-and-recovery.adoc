= Issue 0014: Maintenance and Recovery
:issue-id: 0014
:type: Story
:status: open
:priority: Must
:refs: UC-05, UC-07, BR-01, BR-02, BR-03, BR-04

== Description
Block items at the maintenance threshold and let the technician complete maintenance or permanently retire an unrepairable item.

== Acceptance Criteria
[source,gherkin]
----
Given inspection completes a loan at the category maintenance interval
When the item outcome is determined
Then the item becomes DUE_FOR_MAINTENANCE
And it cannot be issued or reserved
Given an item is due for maintenance with no active loan or inspection
When maintenance is completed
Then the usage counter resets to zero
And the item becomes AVAILABLE
And an audit entry is created
Given an item cannot be repaired
When the technician retires it
Then it becomes RETIRED permanently
And issue, reservation, and maintenance completion are rejected
----
